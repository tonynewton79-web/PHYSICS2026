\begin{abstract}
We analyze first-order reductions of the Einstein equations coupled to pressureless dust and an independently tilted perfect radiation fluid. Conserved dust material mass, a prescribed-metric radiation prolongation, and homogeneous frozen Weyl--matter subsidiaries provide exact restricted controls; a fully coupled Einstein-equivalent reduction is not established. We show that several seemingly natural partial gradient completions are non-symmetrizable. In particular, differentiating the radiation-sourced Weyl evolution while leaving radiation-stress gradients unprolonged yields a concrete $22\times22$ principal symbol with zero eigenvalue of algebraic multiplicity eight and geometric multiplicity seven. This off-shell Jordan defect persists for nonzero radiation coupling even though each diagonal sector is symmetric hyperbolic. A complete first-jet prolongation of any already closed symmetric-hyperbolic system instead inherits a positive block symmetrizer; a $132$-field restricted model illustrates this standard principle. We also derive covariant curvature- and torsion-defect identities and exact dust-gradient compatibility relations. These results delimit a proposed $58$-variable frame construction without asserting a nonlinear Einstein existence theorem: its complete off-shell sources and geometric constraint subsystem remain to be derived.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and relation to existing work}
The Cauchy problem for the Einstein equations with relativistic matter is known to admit several hyperbolic reductions. The relevant issue here is not whether pressureless dust or a single radiation fluid can be evolved individually; both admit substantial existing analyses. Rather, it is whether a \emph{specified} common first-order reduction can treat a geodesic dust material frame, an independently moving radiation fluid, the full gravitational field, and all geometric and matter constraints with a positive symmetrizer.

Friedrich's Lagrangian Einstein--fluid reduction includes pressure-free dust \cite{Friedrich}. Normann and Valiente Kroon \cite{NormannVK} construct a broad orthonormal-frame Einstein--matter reduction with constraint propagation, but use a purely spatial auxiliary matter stress in the chosen flow frame to eliminate derivative couplings. In a dust frame, generically tilted radiation carries nonzero momentum flux, so applying that particular construction unchanged requires additional justification. Fournodavlos, Marshall and Oliynyk's 2026 two-fluid analysis \cite{FMO} addresses a distinct positive-sound-speed regime; it cannot be quoted as the desired pressureless-dust/radiation theorem. These works strongly motivate, rather than settle, the present reduction-specific question.

The preceding short manuscript established the prescribed-metric material construction and a frozen 36-component Weyl subsidiary. We derive the 40-field time-jet extension, examine a proposed 58-variable principal skeleton, audit curved Bianchi compatibility, identify dust-gradient and anisotropic shear obstructions, and test the remaining differentiated Weyl-to-radiation coupling. The dust regularity issue has independent established treatments, including the CMC--spatial-harmonic dust estimates of Fajman, Ofner and Wyatt \cite{FOW}; the present auxiliary-jet calculations do not displace that prior art. No priority or original-gravity theorem is asserted.

\section{Exact Einstein and two-fluid setting}
Use signature $(-,+,+,+)$, $\kap=8\pi G$ and
\begin{align}
G_{ab}+\Lambda g_{ab}&=\kap(T^d_{ab}+T^r_{ab}),\label{eq:einstein}\\
T^d_{ab}&=\rho_d u^d_a u^d_b,\qquad
T^r_{ab}=\tfrac43\rho_r u^r_a u^r_b+\tfrac13\rho_r g_{ab},\label{eq:stress}\\
\nabla^aT^d_{ab}&=0,\qquad\nabla^aT^r_{ab}=0,\quad
\rho_d,\rho_r>0,\quad g(u^d,u^d)=g(u^r,u^r)=-1.\nonumber
\end{align}
The radiation component is a barotropic perfect fluid, not a kinetic photon Boltzmann or Thomson scattering system. Each species is separately conserved, with gravity as their interaction. The discussion of material coordinates assumes irrotational geodesic dust before intersecting dust trajectories; it is not a global assertion for vortical or caustic-forming flows.

\subsection{Pressureless Euler defect and material repair}
Linearize the longitudinal one-dimensional pressureless Euler subsystem around a comoving background with density contrast $\delta_d$ and velocity $v_d$. The principal equations are
\[\partial_t\delta_d+\partial_xv_d=0,\qquad\partial_tv_d=0.\]
The directional matrix $J_0=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ has one eigenvector for its double eigenvalue zero. If $H=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{smallmatrix}\right)>0$ and $HJ_0=(HJ_0)^T$, then symmetry forces $a=0$, a contradiction. Hence the standard Eulerian dust block is not symmetrizable. Introducing a positive squared sound speed $q$ yields $J_q=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\q&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and $H_q=\operatorname{diag}(q,1)$; its condition number grows as $q^{-1}$ when $q\downarrow0$. This is a failure of those variables, not a no-go theorem for dust in every gauge.

\begin{proposition}[Exact material mass conservation]\label{prop:mass}
If $u_d=\partial_\tau$ and the metric on a pre-caustic irrotational dust patch is
\[g=-d\tau^2+h_{ij}(\tau,X)dX^idX^j,\]
then
\[m(X)=\rho_d\sqrt{\det h},\qquad \partial_\tau m=0,
\qquad\rho_d=\frac{m(X)}{\sqrt{\det h}}.\]
With $K_{ij}=-\tfrac12\partial_\tau h_{ij}$ and $K=h^{ij}K_{ij}$,
\[\partial_\tau\rho_d=K\rho_d,\qquad
\partial_i\rho_d=\frac{\partial_i m}{\sqrt{\det h}}-\frac{\rho_d}{2}h^{jk}\partial_i h_{jk}.\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}The dust continuity equation reads $\partial_\tau\rho_d+\rho_d\partial_\tau\log\sqrt{\det h}=0$; the remaining identities follow by differentiation of the determinant.\end{proof}

\subsection{Radiation first-order positivity}
Put $c_s=1/\sqrt3$ and $\sigma=(\sqrt3/4)\log\rho_r$. Relativistic Euler takes the covariant form
\begin{align}
u_r^a\nabla_a\sigma+c_s\nabla_a u_r^a&=0,\\
u_r^a\nabla_a u_r^b+c_s(g^{ba}+u_r^bu_r^a)\nabla_a\sigma&=0.\label{eq:euler}
\end{align}
In a radiation-orthonormal tangent frame for the four physical state components, the symmetric rest-frame principal matrices are $A^0=I_4$ and
\[A^i=c_sS_i,\qquad S_i=\begin{pmatrix}0&e_i^T\\e_i&0_{3\times3}\end{pmatrix}.\]
For dust-frame tilt $u_r=\gamma(e_0+v^ie_i)$, $\gamma=(1-|v|^2)^{-1/2}$, a symmetric coefficient representation is
\begin{align}
R^0&=\gamma\Big(I_4+c_s\sum_iv_iS_i\Big),\label{eq:R0}\\
R^j&=\gamma v^j I_4+c_s\sum_i\Big(\delta_i{}^j+\frac{\gamma-1}{|v|^2}v_i v^j\Big)S_i,\label{eq:Rj}
\end{align}
with the smooth limit at $v=0$. Its time eigenvalues are $\gamma,\gamma,\gamma(1\pm c_s|v|)$, and
\begin{equation}\lambda_{\min}(R^0)=\frac{1-c_s|v|}{\sqrt{1-|v|^2}}
\ge\sqrt{1-c_s^2}=\sqrt{\frac23},\label{eq:acousticbound}\end{equation}
because $(1-c_st)^2-(1-c_s^2)(1-t^2)=(t-c_s)^2$. This is a lower coercivity estimate; upper uniform bounds on the coefficient matrices require a compact subluminal state region.

\section{Prescribed-metric radiation-gradient prolongation}
\begin{proposition}[Symmetric prolonged matter system]\label{prop:prolong}
Let $g$ be a prescribed smooth Lorentzian metric, let $Q$ be smooth positive-density radiation state coordinates with $|v|<1$, and suppose relativistic Euler is written
\[A^0(Q,x)\partial_t Q+A^j(Q,x)\partial_jQ=f(Q,x),\quad
(A^a)^T=A^a,\quad A^0\ge\delta I.\]
For spatial gradient variables $W_i=\partial_iQ$, the differentiated equations can be written with principal part
\[A^0\partial_tW_i+A^j\partial_jW_i=F_i(Q,W,g,\partial g,\partial^2g),\]
where $F_i$ contains no derivatives of $W$. The $W_i$ principal is three copies of the Euler principal. The defining constraints $Z_i=W_i-\partial_iQ$ propagate by a homogeneous linear symmetric-hyperbolic system about a smooth solution.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Differentiate Euler in $x^i$ and replace each first state derivative by $W$. Products of $\partial_iA^a$ with $\partial_aQ$ are algebraic in $(Q,W)$; given smooth $g$, derivatives of prescribed coefficients are known sources. The difference between the differentiated Euler equation and the declared $W_i$ equation is a linear homogeneous equation for $Z_i$. Positive-definite $A^0$ and uniqueness imply preservation of $Z_i=0$.\end{proof}
\begin{remark}The $\partial^2g$ terms are harmless only because $g$ is prescribed. If $g$ is dynamical, classifying these as lower order without new geometric variables or a derivative-counting argument would be circular.\end{remark}

\section{Frozen sourceful two-fluid Weyl subsidiary}
Here only, work in a constant-coefficient tangent model with Minkowski principal metric; constant dust/radiation source parameters are \emph{not} asserted to be a flat nonlinear Einstein solution. Take $U=(\sigma,v_1,v_2,v_3)$, $c_s^2=1/3$, and
\begin{equation}
\partial_t\sigma+c_s\partial^iv_i=0,\qquad
\partial_tv_i+c_s\partial_i\sigma=0.\label{eq:acoustic}
\end{equation}
Introduce independent radiation spatial jets $W_i=(S_i,V_{ij})$ and dust gradient $P_i$:
\begin{equation}\partial_tS_i=-c_s\partial^jV_{ij},\quad
\partial_tV_{ij}=-c_s\partial_jS_i,\quad \partial_t P_i=0,\quad\partial_t d=0.\label{eq:jets}\end{equation}
For symmetric tracefree (STF) tensors $E,H$, put
\[(\curl_{\rm STF}X)_{ij}=\tfrac12(\epsilon_{k\ell i}\partial^kX_{j\ell}+\epsilon_{k\ell j}\partial^k X_{i\ell}),\]
and take the sourced ten-equation electric--magnetic Weyl evolution
\begin{equation}
\partial_tE+\curl_{\rm STF}H=-b\,\STF(V),\qquad
\partial_tH-\curl_{\rm STF}E=0,\quad b=\frac{2\kap\bar\rho_r}{3}.\label{eq:weyl}
\end{equation}
The constraints are
\begin{align}
\cC_i^E&=\partial^jE_{ij}-2bc_sS_i-\frac\kap3P_i,\\
\cC_i^H&=\partial^jH_{ij}-b\epsilon_{ijk}V_{jk},\label{eq:constraints}\end{align}
with $Z_i^r=W_i-\partial_iU$, $F_{ij}^r=\partial_iW_j-\partial_jW_i$, $Z_i^d=P_i-\partial_id$, $F_{ij}^d=\partial_iP_j-\partial_jP_i$. The component counts are $6+12+12+3+3=36$.

\begin{theorem}[Frozen 36-component homogeneous subsidiary]\label{thm:36}
On $\TT^3$ (or rapidly decreasing data on $\RR^3$), these 36 constraints satisfy
\begin{align}
\partial_t\cC_i^E+\tfrac12(\curl\cC^H)_i&=\tfrac b3\sum_j(F_{ij}^r)_{v_j},\label{eq:ce}\\
\partial_t\cC_i^H-\tfrac12(\curl\cC^E)_i&=\tfrac\kap{12}\epsilon_{ijk}F_{jk}^d,\label{eq:ch}\\
\partial_t Z_i^r+R^k\partial_kZ_i^r&=0,\quad
\partial_tF_{ij}^r+R^k\partial_kF_{ij}^r=0,\nonumber\\
\partial_t Z_i^d&=0,\quad \partial_tF_{ij}^d=0,\nonumber
\end{align}
where $R^k=c_sS_k$. There is a constant positive symmetrizer and, with
\[\mathcal E_s=\tfrac12(\|\cC^E\|_{H^s}^2+\|\cC^H\|_{H^s}^2+
\sum_i\|Z_i^r\|_{H^s}^2+\sum_{i<j}\|F_{ij}^r\|_{H^s}^2+
\sum_i\|Z_i^d\|_{H^s}^2+\sum_{i<j}\|F_{ij}^d\|_{H^s}^2),\]
we have
\begin{equation}
\mathcal E_s(t)\le e^{K_*|t|}\mathcal E_s(0),\qquad
K_*=\max\left\{\frac{\sqrt2|b|}{3},\frac{|\kap|}{6}\right\}.\label{eq:E36}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For STF $X$, $\diver(\curl_{\rm STF}X)=\tfrac12\curl(\diver X)$. Differentiate \eqref{eq:constraints} and use \eqref{eq:jets}--\eqref{eq:weyl}; $c_s^2=1/3$ cancels the would-be second derivatives. The surviving radiation term is $b(\partial_iV_{jj}-\partial_jV_{ij})/3$, a contraction of $F^r$. The remaining magnetic source is $\kap\epsilon_{ijk}F_{jk}^d/12$ and must not be omitted for independent dust jets. The curl and defining constraints commute with the constant acoustic operator. The opposite-curl Weyl blocks are symmetric. With the canonical three-index curl-source maps $G_rG_r^T=2I_3$ and $G_dG_d^T=I_3$, integrate by parts and apply $2xy\le x^2+y^2$ to obtain $|\mathcal E_s'|\le K_*\mathcal E_s$.\end{proof}

The 24 contracted Weyl Bianchi components of this frozen model were also independently reconstructed by tensor algebra. This does not establish all nonlinear frame, gauge, Einstein, torsion or connection constraint equations.

\section{Radiation time jets and the 40-component extension}
In the frozen comoving system, evolve the independent fourth radiation jet $W_0$ by the same acoustic operator as each $W_i$, and define the time-jet residual
\[J=W_0+\sum_j R^jW_j.\]
\begin{proposition}[Exact frozen time-jet curl closure]\label{prop:40}
Let $B^j$ be \emph{constant} matrices, with $\partial_tW_a+B^j\partial_jW_a=0$ for $a=0,1,2,3$. Set $J=W_0+B^jW_j$, $F_{ij}=\partial_iW_j-\partial_jW_i$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:timejet}
(\partial_t+B^k\partial_k)J
=\sum_{i<j}[B^i,B^j]F_{ij}.
\end{equation}
For the comoving radiation matrices $B^j=c_s S_j$ this is an algebraic coupling to the earlier radiation curl constraints. Together with Theorem~\ref{thm:36}, it yields a 40-component constant-coefficient symmetric-hyperbolic homogeneous subsidiary. The same closure mechanism applies to any frozen tilted symmetric-hyperbolic Euler matrices $A^0>0$, $A^j=(A^j)^T$ using $B^j=(A^0)^{-1}A^j$ and symmetrizer $A^0$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Apply $\partial_t+B^k\partial_k$ to $J$. The noncommutation of $B^k$ and $B^j$ gives $\sum_{k,j}[B^k,B^j]\partial_kW_j$, which antisymmetrizes to the right-hand side of \eqref{eq:timejet}. This is lower order in the enlarged constraint system because $F$ is already a constraint variable.\end{proof}
For the specified comoving normalization, an energy estimate is available with $K_{40}=\max\{(\sqrt2/3)\sqrt{b^2+1},|\kap|/6\}$, as reproduced by the 40-variable exact verification. This numerical coefficient should not be reused under a different scaling of the time-jet constraint without transforming the energy accordingly. Neither this result nor the full time-jet count is a nonlinear, curved Einstein constraint theorem.

\section{Conditional Einstein Hamiltonian--momentum subsidiary}
For comparison with the geometric reduction, define the physical ADM constraint residuals
\[\mathcal H={}^{(3)}R+K^2-K_{ij}K^{ij}-2\kap\rho_{\rm total}-2\Lambda,
\qquad\mathcal M_i=D_j(K^j{}_i-\delta^j_iK)-\kap j_i.\]
Given the complete spatial Einstein evolution equations, unit lapse, zero shift and separate conservation of both matter stresses, the standard frozen-principal constraint subsidiary has the form
\[\partial_t\mathcal H+2\partial^i\mathcal M_i\simeq0,
\qquad\partial_t\mathcal M_i+\tfrac12\partial_i\mathcal H\simeq0.\]
The four-variable spatial matrices are symmetrized by $\operatorname{diag}(1,4,4,4)>0$; in a unit direction the speeds are $\pm1,0,0$. This is a \emph{conditional established} consequence of the Einstein/Bianchi equations, not the missing off-shell tetrad/connection subsidiary for the 58 variables. In particular it cannot be appended to the 40-field frozen system without checking the geometric cross-derivative terms.

\section{A 58-variable candidate frame and radiation principal skeleton}
Fix a geodesic, irrotational dust frame $e_0=u_d=\partial_t$, Fermi propagate the spatial frame, and impose $\Gamma_{0ab}=0$. The proposed auxiliary state is
\begin{equation}
V=(e_i{}^j\;[9],\ \Gamma_{iab}\;[18],\ \rho_d\;[1],\ E,H\;[10],\ Q\;[4],\ W_a\;[16]),\qquad \dim V=58.\label{eq:58}
\end{equation}
The first 28 variables are intended to evolve through the dust-frame and temporal Ricci identities. The Weyl equations have the standard symmetric STF curl principal, while radiation and its four covariant first jets use five acoustic blocks. At a dust-frame point, introduce STF basis matrices
\[\begin{aligned}
B_1&=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0), & B_2&=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2),\\
B_3&=e_1e_2^T+e_2e_1^T, & B_4&=e_1e_3^T+e_3e_1^T,\\
B_5&=e_2e_3^T+e_3e_2^T.
\end{aligned}\]
with Gram $D=\operatorname{diag}(2,6,2,2,2)$ and STF curl matrices $C_j$ satisfying $DC_j=-C_j^TD$. In weighted variables define
\[H_W=\operatorname{diag}(D,D),\qquad
\mathbb W_j=\begin{pmatrix}0&DC_j\\-DC_j&0\end{pmatrix}=\mathbb W_j^T.\]
The proposed principal coefficients are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:58A}
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathbb A^0_{\rm cand}&=\operatorname{diag}(I_{28},H_W,R^0,R^0,R^0,R^0,R^0),\\
\mathbb A^j_{\rm cand}&=\operatorname{diag}(0_{28},\mathbb W_j,R^j,R^j,R^j,R^j,R^j).
\end{aligned}}
\end{equation}
Every displayed $\mathbb A^j_{\rm cand}$ is symmetric, with $\mathbb A^0_{\rm cand}\ge\sqrt{2/3}\,I_{58}$. With frame coefficients $e_i{}^k$, each coordinate spatial symbol is the symmetric linear combination $\sum_i e_i{}^k\mathbb A^i_{\rm cand}$. This verifies the algebra of a \emph{candidate principal skeleton} and does not provide a complete source list or prove that the proposed equations coincide off shell with a specified Einstein reduction.

Covariant radiation first jets $W_b=\cD_bQ$ are attractive because the commutator $[\cD_a,\cD_b]Q$ can be represented through algebraic curvature plus a curvature defect, instead of leaving bare connection derivatives. However, an off-shell auxiliary system must also evolve the defining, curvature and torsion defects consistently. Block positivity for \eqref{eq:58A} cannot substitute for this verification. In particular, the pre-caustic mass formula does not imply that every spatial derivative of dust density can be expressed algebraically in the 58 variables; Proposition~\ref{prop:gradientgap} below gives a torsion-free counterexample.

\section{Curved geometric and matter zero quantities}
Let $\theta^a$ be the coframe and $\omega^a{}_b$ a metric-compatible connection. Introduce an algebraic Riemann curvature model $\mathfrak R^a{}_b$ built from $(E,H,T^d,T^r)$ and obeying the usual algebraic Riemann symmetries. Set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:zero}
\Sigma^a=d\theta^a+\omega^a{}_b\wedge\theta^b,\qquad
\Delta^a{}_b=R(\omega)^a{}_b-\mathfrak R^a{}_b,\qquad
\cB^a{}_b=D\mathfrak R^a{}_b.
\end{equation}
The last expression is the \emph{full matrix-valued differential Bianchi residual}; it should not be confused, without an explicit algebraic projection, with the six sourced Weyl divergence constraints $\cC^E,\cC^H$ from the frozen model.

\begin{proposition}[Cartan transport identities]\label{prop:cartan}
The exact exterior identities are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cartan}
D\Sigma^a=\Delta^a{}_b\wedge\theta^b
+\mathfrak R^a{}_b\wedge\theta^b,\qquad
D\Delta^a{}_b=-\cB^a{}_b.
\end{equation}
When the algebraic first Bianchi identity $\mathfrak R^a{}_b\wedge\theta^b=0$ holds, the first equation reduces to $D\Sigma=\Delta\wedge\theta$. If the reduced equations impose $\Sigma_{0i}=\Delta_{0i}=0$ exactly, their spatial components have conditional transport principal parts, sourced by spatial zero quantities and $\cB$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Apply $D$ to $D\theta=\Sigma$, use $D^2\theta=R(\omega)\wedge\theta$, and insert $R(\omega)=\mathfrak R+\Delta$. The second identity follows from $DR(\omega)=0$.\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Off-shell curvature- and torsion-adjusted radiation curl]\label{prop:jetcurl}
For a vector radiation state $u^a$, independent jets $W_b{}^a$, and defining constraint $Z_b{}^a=W_b{}^a-\nabla_bu^a$, put
\[\cF_{cb}{}^a=\nabla_cW_b{}^a-\nabla_bW_c{}^a-
\mathfrak R^a{}_{dcb}u^d.\]
If $\Sigma^e{}_{cb}$ denotes the torsion of the independent connection, the exact identity is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:torsioncurl}
\boxed{\cF_{cb}{}^a=
\nabla_cZ_b{}^a-\nabla_bZ_c{}^a+
\Delta^a{}_{dcb}u^d-\Sigma^e{}_{cb}\nabla_eu^a.}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Subtract the two covariant second derivatives of $u$ and use the Ricci commutator with torsion,
$[\nabla_c,\nabla_b]u^a=R(\omega)^a{}_{dcb}u^d-\Sigma^e{}_{cb}\nabla_eu^a$.\end{proof}
The same construction applies to the state bundle of $Q=(\sigma,u)$, with its induced tangent connection and algebraic fiber-curvature terms. The geometric defect cannot in general be deleted by demanding an ordinary curl $\nabla_{[c}W_{b]}=0$. A curved expanding FLRW dust--radiation control provides a nonzero commutator witness (Appendix~\ref{app:flrw}).

\section{Attack 009: full Bianchi identity and its off-diagonal principal term}
\begin{theorem}[Exact full-Bianchi defect coupling]\label{thm:fullB}
For any matrix-valued curvature two-form model $\mathfrak R$ and any connection one-form $\omega$, define $\Delta=R(\omega)-\mathfrak R$ and $\cB=D\mathfrak R$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:DB}
\boxed{D\cB=[\Delta,\mathfrak R],}
\end{equation}
where the graded commutator of two two-forms is $[X,Y]=X\wedge Y-Y\wedge X$. In particular the source is algebraic in the curvature-definition defect and the curvature model. This is a standard identity in connection theory, not a priority or novelty claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}For any endomorphism-valued form, $D^2\mathfrak R=[R(\omega),\mathfrak R]$. Using $R(\omega)=\mathfrak R+\Delta$ and the identity $[\mathfrak R,\mathfrak R]=0$ gives \eqref{eq:DB}. No field equations are needed.\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact dependence on temporal Bianchi residuals]\label{cor:spatialB}
In a local dust-adapted coordinate frame, modulo connection-coefficient terms with no derivatives of $\cB$, the $0123$ component of \eqref{eq:DB} reads
\begin{equation}\label{eq:spatialB}
\boxed{\partial_t\cB_{123}
-\partial_1\cB_{023}+\partial_2\cB_{013}-\partial_3\cB_{012}
\simeq0.}
\end{equation}
Suppose a \emph{specified} reduced evolution yields $\cB_{0ij}=L_{ij}{}^{\alpha}(V)\cC_\alpha$ modulo terms with no derivatives of $\cC$. Then the spatial Bianchi row of its constraint principal matrices contains the exact off-diagonal coefficients
\begin{equation}\label{eq:offdiag}
(B^1_{\rm full})_{\cB_{123},\alpha}=-L_{23}{}^{\alpha},\quad
(B^2_{\rm full})_{\cB_{123},\alpha}=+L_{13}{}^{\alpha},\quad
(B^3_{\rm full})_{\cB_{123},\alpha}=-L_{12}{}^{\alpha},
\end{equation}
up to the common normalization of the $\cB$ row and its time coefficient.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}For a three-form, $(D\cB)_{0123}=D_0\cB_{123}-D_1\cB_{023}+D_2\cB_{013}-D_3\cB_{012}$. Inserting the declared temporal expressions and collecting the first derivatives of constraints proves the displayed coefficients.\end{proof}

\textbf{Interpretation.} The conditional transport picture for $\Sigma$ and $\Delta$ does not establish that the Bianchi residual has a zero spatial principal block. Such a claim would be valid only after independently proving $\cB_{0ij}=0$ as an \emph{identity of the selected reduced off-shell equations}, not merely after restricting to physical solutions. The 58-field candidate has not supplied the complete $L_{ij}{}^{\alpha}$, so \eqref{eq:offdiag} is an explicit set of \emph{requirements}, not a fabricated numerical matrix.

\section{Constraint-adjustment nonuniqueness and characteristic resonance}
The full off-shell subsidiary depends on constraint additions to the reduced evolution. This is familiar in hyperbolic Einstein reductions, but it is especially important when characteristic speeds coincide.

\begin{proposition}[Same constrained dynamics, different off-shell hyperbolicity]\label{prop:underdet}
Fix real $a$ and $\alpha\ne0$ and consider auxiliary variables $(g,q,w)$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:toy}
q_t+a q_x=0,\qquad w_t+a w_x=0,\qquad
g_t=\alpha(w-q_x).
\end{equation}
The constraint $Z=w-q_x$ obeys $Z_t+aZ_x=0$. For every initially constrained solution, $Z=0$ and $g_t=0$. Therefore the $\alpha\ne0$ and $\alpha=0$ systems induce the same constrained dynamics. Nevertheless, at $a=0$ the off-shell principal matrix for $\alpha\ne0$ is
\[B(0)=\begin{pmatrix}0&\alpha&0\\0&0&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix},\]
which has a nontrivial Jordan block and admits no positive symmetrizer, whereas the $\alpha=0$ principal matrix is identically zero and symmetrizable.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Differentiate the equation for $q$ and subtract from the $w$ equation to get the homogeneous equation for $Z$. The principal matrix follows by writing $g_t+\alpha q_x=\alpha w$. For $\alpha\ne0$, $B^2=0$ and $\operatorname{rank}B=1$, so it is not diagonalizable. A real matrix self-adjoint for a positive inner product is diagonalizable, giving the claimed impossibility.\end{proof}

For the collinear radiation sound characteristic in dust-frame coordinates,

\begin{lemma}[Projected triangular resonance obstruction]\label{lem:res}

Here $P_A,P_D$ are spectral projectors onto the $\lambda$ eigenspaces.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}The projected forcing of the $D$-eigenvectors into the $A$-eigenspace is resonant with the same eigenvalue and creates a nontrivial Jordan chain. Equivalently the equation $(A-\lambda I)x=-Cy$ has no solution for some $y\in\ker(D-\lambda I)$ when $P_ACy\ne0$.\end{proof}

\section{Sourceful contracted Bianchi projections and dust-gradient completion}\label{sec:a10}
This section distinguishes the contracted Weyl--Bianchi residual from the full matrix-valued curvature three-form of Theorem~\ref{thm:fullB}. Fix signature $(-,+,+,+)$ and an oriented orthonormal frame at a point; all principal statements are evaluated in its normal-frame approximation. Write

\begin{theorem}[Algebraic sourceful Bianchi reconstruction]\label{thm:B24}
Let $\epsilon_{123}=1$, and define
\begin{align*}
p_i&=\mathfrak B_{00i},&q_i&=\tfrac12\epsilon_{ijk}\mathfrak B_{0jk},\\

\begin{equation}\label{eq:Bcyctrace}
\mathfrak B_{bcd}=-\mathfrak B_{bdc},\qquad

g^{bc}\mathfrak B_{bcd}=-\frac\kap2 j_d
\end{equation}
are equivalent to the reconstruction
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Breconstruct}
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mathfrak B_{00i}&=p_i,&\mathfrak B_{0ij}&=\epsilon_{ijk}q_k,\\
\mathfrak B_{i0j}&=S^E_{ij}+\frac{\kap}{6}j_0\delta_{ij}

 -\frac\kap3\nabla j_0+\diver S^E,\\

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The cyclic identity follows from the algebraic Weyl identity and the cyclic Schouten curl. Tracing the Weyl term gives zero. Tracing the Schouten terms gives $-\nabla^b L_{bd}+\nabla_d\operatorname{tr}L=-\kap j_d/2$, using the displayed expression for $L$. Let $X_{ij}=\mathfrak B_{i0j}$ and $Y_{i\ell}=\epsilon_{\ell jk}\mathfrak B_{ijk}/2$. The cyclic identity implies $X_{ij}-X_{ji}=\epsilon_{ijk}q_k$ and $\operatorname{tr}Y=0$. Insert~\eqref{eq:Breconstruct}, select $(c,d)=(0,i)$ and dualize $(c,d)=(i,j)$ with $\epsilon_{kij}/2$. The resulting six identities are~\eqref{eq:Bprincipal}. Their spatial curl blocks are mutually adjoint. Curved covariant commutators contribute lower-order curvature/defect sources and are not evaluated by this principal calculation.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A dust-gradient nonalgebraicity witness]\label{prop:gradientgap}

\begin{proof}The mass law follows from $\sqrt{\det h}=1/\det F$. Differentiate the determinant using Jacobi's identity. For the example, $d\theta^1=0$ despite arbitrary $\alpha$, and $\rho_d=m(1+\alpha x)$. The asserted pointwise values and different spatial gradients follow directly. \begin{proposition}[Failure of the unadjusted dust-gradient augmentation]\label{prop:dustjordan}
Suppose the spatial frame and connection retain the dust-geodesic transport principal part, so that the expansion $\Theta=\Gamma_{i0}{}^i$ has no spatial derivative in its evolution equation. Introduce three independent dust-gradient variables $p_i^d=e_i(\rho_d)$ and evolve them by differentiation of the exact dust mass equation, \emph{without any further constraint adjustments}. If $\partial_t e_i=-\Gamma_{i0}{}^k e_k$ and $\partial_t\rho_d=-\rho_d\Theta$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pdtransport}
\partial_t p_i^d=-\Gamma_{i0}{}^k p_k^d-\Theta p_i^d-\rho_d\,e_i\Theta.
\end{equation}
At a frozen state with $\rho_d>0$ and a unit Fourier direction $n$, the transport-only subsystem containing $\Theta$ and $p^d_n$ has directional symbol
\begin{equation}\label{eq:badjordan}
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\\rho_d&0\end{pmatrix},}
\end{equation}
which is nonzero nilpotent and cannot admit a positive symmetrizer. Consequently the unadjusted augmentation of the candidate block-diagonal gravitational transport system by these three natural dust-gradient equations fails symmetric hyperbolicity. This does \emph{not} prohibit constraint-adjusted reductions, a different principal coupling, or mixed-regularity methods.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The commutator $[e_0,e_i]=-\Gamma_{i0}{}^k e_k$ gives
$e_0e_i(\rho_d)=-\Gamma_{i0}{}^k e_k(\rho_d)+e_i(-\rho_d\Theta)$, proving~\eqref{eq:pdtransport}. In the declared transport principal, $\partial_t\Theta\simeq0$ and $\partial_t p_n^d+\rho_d\partial_n\Theta\simeq0$. The matrix~\eqref{eq:badjordan} squares to zero but has rank one. Any matrix self-adjoint in a positive inner product is diagonalizable; this matrix is not. Equivalently, symmetrizing it by a positive $2\times2$ matrix forces its lower-right diagonal entry to vanish, a contradiction.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why this does not contradict the earlier frozen Weyl system]

A proposed 58-variable Einstein-equivalent reduction must prove that these constraint derivatives close without the naive nilpotent block, rather than assuming that a connection-algebraic formula for $e_i(\rho_d)$ exists.
\end{remark}


\section{Paired dust gradients and the anisotropic shear-jet obstruction}\label{sec:a11}
The counterexample in Proposition~\ref{prop:dustjordan} applies to evolving the spatial dust gradient while leaving the expansion as an unprolonged transport variable. A minimal first response is to carry both gradients as independent auxiliary fields. The following calculation gives the exact gain and also the next limitation; it does not assume a complete Einstein frame reduction.

Let $e_0=u_d$ be geodesic and hypersurface-orthogonal. On the pre-caustic patch choose a spatial orthonormal triad parallel-transported along $e_0$. Decompose
\begin{equation}\label{eq:thetaS}
\nabla_i (u_d)_j=S_{ij}=\tfrac13\Theta\delta_{ij}+\sigma_{ij},\quad
\sigma_{ij}=\sigma_{ji},\quad \sigma^j{}_j=0,\quad

The $Y$-jet is symmetric tracefree in its last two indices and therefore has fifteen components before further identities are applied. Here $r,V$ may be related to the radiation covariant jets used earlier, but equality is a separate defining constraint.

If the above definitions and the dust-frame commutator hold on a smooth Einstein--dust--radiation solution, then

\section{First-jet closure and a resonant Weyl--radiation obstruction}\label{sec:resonant}
The previous dust-gradient tests show why adding isolated derivatives to transport fields may destroy diagonalizability. The Weyl--radiation coupling gives a second, independent mechanism. It is already present in the exact comoving frozen model, without gravitational-frame coefficient derivatives.

Fix a unit wave covector $n=e^1$, radiation sound speed $c_s^2=1/3$, and a nonzero radiation source coefficient $b$. Consider the Weyl evolution
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weql12}
\partial_tE+\curl_{\mathrm{STF}}H=-b\,\STF(V),\qquad
\partial_tH-\curl_{\mathrm{STF}}E=0,
\end{equation}
where the independently evolved radiation first jet is $W_i=(S_i,V_{i1},V_{i2},V_{i3})$ and obeys the three acoustic blocks
$\partial_tW_i+R^k\partial_kW_i=0$.
Promote the single directional Weyl jets $X_E=\partial_1E$, $X_H=\partial_1H$ to independent ten-component variables, but \emph{do not} promote derivatives of the radiation jets $W_i$. Differentiating~\eqref{eq:weql12} then gives a principal derivative $-b\STF(\partial_1V)$ in the $X_E$ equation. In the five-element trace-free basis of Section 4, the $22$-variable directional symbol is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:B22}
B_{22}(e^1)=
\begin{pmatrix}
W(e^1)&bT\\
0&\operatorname{diag}(R(e^1),R(e^1),R(e^1))
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad T=\begin{pmatrix}S\\0_{5\times12}\end{pmatrix},
\end{equation}
where $W(e^1)$ is the ten-field Weyl curl matrix, $S$ maps the twelve radiation first-jet coordinates to the five trace-free coefficients of $\STF(V)$, and $R(e^1)$ is the symmetric acoustic principal matrix.

\begin{theorem}[Exact defect of the incomplete Weyl--radiation jet completion]\label{thm:a12jordan}
For every $b\ne0$, the matrix $B_{22}(e^1)$ has characteristic polynomial
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p22}
\chi_{22}(z)=z^8(z^2-1)^2(z^2-\tfrac14)^2(z^2-\tfrac13)^3,
\end{equation}
and its kernel has dimension seven. Thus its zero eigenvalue has algebraic multiplicity eight and geometric multiplicity seven. No positive definite symmetrizer can make this $22$-field off-shell principal block symmetric. The failure is specific to the unadjusted partial prolongation and is not an obstruction to the on-shell Einstein--matter equations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The Weyl block has characteristic polynomial $z^2(z^2-1)^2(z^2-1/4)^2$ and a two-dimensional kernel, consisting of one longitudinal electric and one longitudinal magnetic mode. Each radiation acoustic block has eigenvalues $0,0,\pm1/\sqrt3$; hence the radiation direct sum has a six-dimensional kernel. Because~\eqref{eq:B22} is block upper triangular, these facts give~\eqref{eq:p22}.

It remains to test the off-diagonal coupling at the common zero speed. Choose the longitudinal electric zero mode $E_L=\operatorname{diag}(-2,1,1)$. Its $D$-weighted pairing with the STF source is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:proj12}
\langle E_L,\STF(V)\rangle=\operatorname{tr}(E_LV)
=-2V_{11}+V_{22}+V_{33}.
\end{equation}
In the zero eigenspace of the radiation symbol along $e^1$, the velocity components $V_{i1}$ vanish, whereas $V_{i2},V_{i3}$ are free. In particular, the choice $V_{22}=1$ and all other entries zero is a radiation zero mode whose projected source~\eqref{eq:proj12} equals one. The longitudinal magnetic zero-mode projection vanishes because $T$ enters only the electric evolution.

The Weyl block is self-adjoint for the positive Gram matrix $H_W$, so the inhomogeneous Weyl zero-mode equation $W(e^1)X=-bTY$ is solvable precisely when its projections onto both longitudinal modes vanish. These impose exactly one independent linear condition, $V_{22}+V_{33}=0$, on the six-dimensional radiation zero eigenspace. Therefore
$\dim\ker B_{22}=2+(6-1)=7$.
A matrix self-adjoint for a positive inner product is diagonalizable; the defective zero eigenvalue excludes such a symmetrizer. Independent exact-rational matrix elimination gives $\operatorname{rank}B_{22}=15$, agreeing with this argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Off-shell versus physical constraints]
A plane-wave radiation jet with $V_{22}$ varying only in the $x^1$ direction does not generally satisfy $W_i=\partial_iQ$. It is included deliberately because a symmetric-hyperbolic auxiliary formulation must act on an open set of off-shell variables, with defining constraints propagated separately. This result does not show that the on-shell reduced Einstein--matter system is defective. Constraint adjustments might remove the derivative coupling without introducing every second jet.
\end{remark}

There is a standard constructive alternative \emph{once a closed first-order symmetric-hyperbolic base equation is already known}. Its precise assumption prevents the argument from being misapplied to the proposed incomplete Einstein-frame system.

\begin{theorem}[Finite complete first-jet prolongation]\label{thm:firstjet}
Let $U\in\mathbb R^m$ solve a smooth quasilinear system on a coordinate patch,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:basehyper}
A^0(U)\partial_tU+\sum_{j=1}^3A^j(U)\partial_jU=F(U),
\qquad A^0=A^{0T}>0,\quad A^j=A^{jT}.
\end{equation}
Suppose these are the \emph{complete} first-order evolution equations, with no omitted derivatives in their sources. Introducing all three independent spatial first jets $Q_i=\partial_iU$ gives a closed $4m$-field quasilinear symmetric-hyperbolic prolongation. Its principal matrices are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:blockjet}
\mathbb A^\mu=\operatorname{diag}(A^\mu,A^\mu,A^\mu,A^\mu),\qquad \mu=0,1,2,3,
\end{equation}
with positive $\mathbb A^0$. The defining constraints $C_i=Q_i-\partial_iU$ propagate homogeneously from compatible initial data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write the algebraic replacement
$\widehat U_t=(A^0)^{-1}(F-\sum_jA^jQ_j)$.
For every $i$, differentiate~\eqref{eq:basehyper} formally and define the prolonged equation by replacing each first derivative on the right by $Q_j$ or $\widehat U_t$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fulljet}
A^0\partial_tQ_i+A^j\partial_jQ_i
=DF[Q_i]-DA^0[Q_i]\widehat U_t
-\sum_j DA^j[Q_i]Q_j.
\end{equation}
The right-hand side is smooth and algebraic in $(U,Q_1,Q_2,Q_3)$, establishing~\eqref{eq:blockjet}. Since~\eqref{eq:basehyper} is imposed, the differences between $(Q_j,\widehat U_t)$ and $(\partial_jU,\partial_tU)$ are algebraic linear combinations of the $C_j$. Subtracting the exact differentiated base equation from~\eqref{eq:fulljet} therefore gives a first-order system for $C_i$ with principal matrices $A^\mu$ and smooth homogeneous zeroth-order terms. Standard symmetric-hyperbolic uniqueness gives $C_i=0$ for compatible initial data. This is the familiar finite prolongation mechanism underlying symmetric-hyperbolic regularity theory \cite{Kato}.
\end{proof}
