\title{Exact Equivalence and Ancilla Contracts in Hadamard--CCZ Quantum Circuits}
\author{Tony Newton}

\begin{abstract}
We give exact controlled-phase circuit identities in the Hadamard--CCZ gate set, explicitly distinguishing initialized from borrowed ancillary qubits. We provide a constructive n-body phase formula, a four-body borrowed-ancilla construction, a unique cubic phase normal form for CCZ-only words, and a parity obstruction for exact equivalence of H--CCZ words. We further construct a uniform proof-carrying compiler for explicitly listed Boolean phase polynomials of degree at least three. A standalone Boolean-algebra checker verifies each emitted gate block, its exact phase, clean/borrowed ancilla restoration and resource counts, independently of the compiler. For shared-prefix families of high-degree monomials we also prove a strictly smaller circuit, relative to the independent-block compiler, by temporarily borrowing a prefix \emph{data} wire without allocating any extra unknown-state qubit. These are restricted constructions with substantial prior art, not globally optimal gate-count or complexity-theoretic lower bounds.
\end{abstract}
\section{Exact gate identities}
For computational basis bits a,b,c, CCZ contributes the phase $(-1)^{abc}$, while $H=2^{-1/2}\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$.
Conjugation on the target gives an exact Toffoli gate:
\[
\mathrm{CCX}_{ab\to t}=H_t\,\mathrm{CCZ}_{abt}\,H_t.
\]
Indeed $H\operatorname{diag}(1,(-1)^{ab})H=X^{ab}$ on either possible control product. The equality holds as an operator, including on arbitrary entangled states.

\begin{theorem}[Hadamard-count parity]
If an H--CCZ circuit contains h Hadamards, then its full matrix is $U=2^{-h/2}M$, with integer entries in M. Two exactly equal nonzero H--CCZ unitary matrices must have Hadamard counts of the same parity. For matching parity, their exact equivalence reduces to comparing integer matrices after multiplication by an appropriate integer power of two.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each Hadamard multiplies amplitudes by $2^{-1/2}$ times an integral matrix; CCZ is an integral diagonal sign matrix. Induction gives the stated factorization. If two equal unitaries had opposite H-count parity, a nonzero entry would be rational and an irrational rational multiple of $\sqrt2$, impossible. All unitary matrices have nonzero entries. For equal parity the normalizing ratio is an integer power of two.
\end{proof}
An irrelevant overall complex phase between real unitaries must be a real sign, so it does not change this parity test. Parity equality alone is not sufficient for equivalence.

\section{A clean-ancilla multi-body phase}
Let $P_n$ act on n data qubits by
\[
P_n|x_0\cdots x_{n-1}\rangle =
(-1)^{x_0x_1\cdots x_{n-1}}|x_0\cdots x_{n-1}\rangle.
\]
For n=3 this is CCZ. For n at least four, initialize n-3 ancillary qubits $a_1,\ldots,a_{n-3}$ to zero. Compute $a_1=x_0x_1$ with Toffoli and then successively compute $a_j=a_{j-1}x_j$ for $2\le j\le n-3$. Apply CCZ to the last ancilla and the final two data wires; finally reverse the entire Toffoli ladder.
\begin{theorem}[Clean-ancilla construction]
For each integer $n\ge3$ the described circuit maps
\[
|\psi\rangle|0^{n-3}\rangle\longmapsto
(P_n|\psi\rangle)|0^{n-3}\rangle
\]
for every data state, using precisely n-3 clean ancillas, 2n-5 CCZ gates, and $4(n-3)$ Hadamards.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On a basis input, induction gives $a_j=\prod_{k=0}^{j}x_k$. The central CCZ therefore contributes exactly the desired n-bit sign. All Toffolis are uncomputed in reverse order, restoring each scratch bit without further phase. The assertion follows for any input by linearity, including external entanglement. There are n-3 Toffolis in each half of the ladder and each Toffoli compiles to one CCZ plus two H; add the central CCZ.
\end{proof}

\begin{tabular}{rrrr}\toprule
Data n & Clean ancillas & CCZ & H\\\midrule
3&0&1&0\\4&1&3&4\\5&2&5&8\\
6&3&7&12\\$n\ge3$&$n-3$&$2n-5$&$4(n-3)$\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{A borrowed-ancilla identity}
Let a,b,c,d be four data wires and z a borrowed auxiliary wire in an arbitrary, possibly externally entangled, state. In temporal order apply
\[
\mathrm{CCX}_{ab\to z},\quad
\mathrm{CCZ}_{zcd},\quad
\mathrm{CCX}_{ab\to z},\quad
\mathrm{CCZ}_{zcd}.
\]
\begin{theorem}[Borrowed-ancilla four-body phase]
The above circuit equals $P_4\otimes I_z$ on all five physical qubits and uses exactly four CCZ and four Hadamard gates after Toffoli decomposition.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first Toffoli toggles z to $z\oplus ab$. The first CCZ contributes $(-1)^{(z\oplus ab)cd}$. The second Toffoli restores z, and the last CCZ contributes $(-1)^{zcd}$. The total exponent modulo two is $abcd$, independent of z. Operator equality on every basis state implies exact restoration for all borrowed quantum inputs, including entanglement.
\end{proof}
Dropping the last CCZ fails: for $(a,b,c,d,z)=(0,0,1,1,1)$ it produces a minus sign where the desired gate produces a plus sign. Substituting a dirty initial scratch bit into the clean ladder also fails.

\section{A diagonal Boolean phase obstruction}
\begin{theorem}[CCZ-only normal form]
The subgroup generated by CCZ gates on distinct triples of n wires is isomorphic to $(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^{\binom n3}$. Its unique phase polynomial is
\[
f(x)=\sum_{S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\},\,|S|=3}
\alpha_S\prod_{j\in S}x_j\quad\bmod2.
\]
No CCZ-only circuit implements $P_n$ for $n\ge4$, even with unused ancillas initially zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Squarefree Boolean monomials form a basis of functions on $\mathbb F_2^n$, with uniqueness by Boolean M\"obius inversion. The cubic monomials indexed by triples are independent, and each CCZ toggles one coefficient. An n-body monomial of degree at least four cannot occur. A gate incident to an ancilla that remains zero contributes no phase on the clean-ancilla input subspace.
\end{proof}
This degree obstruction is only for the CCZ-only sublanguage; it is not a lower bound for all circuits containing Hadamards.


\section{Proof-carrying compilation of phase polynomials}
Let $\mathcal S$ be a finite list of subsets of
$\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$, each of cardinality at least three.
The target is the explicitly given diagonal unitary
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phasefun}
P_f|x\rangle=(-1)^{f(x)}|x\rangle,\qquad
f(x)=\bigoplus_{S\in\mathcal S}\prod_{j\in S}x_j .
\end{equation}
Repeated subsets cancel in pairs over $\mathbb F_2$.
After canonical cancellation let $\mathcal T$ be the
remaining set of distinct monomials.
For each cubic monomial apply one CCZ.
A monomial of degree $k\ge4$ is realized by
the clean-ancilla construction above, or, if
$k=4$, by the borrowed-ancilla construction.
All blocks are concatenated, reusing the
same scratch register only after the preceding
block has restored it.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform resource and ancilla contract]
\label{thm:pcc}
Suppose the explicitly listed monomials in
\eqref{eq:phasefun} all have degree at least three,
and adequate ancillas are provided for the
chosen block implementations. The deterministic
compiler terminates and emits a finite
H--CCZ circuit $C_f$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pcccontract}
C_f\bigl(|\psi\rangle_D|0\rangle_C|\phi\rangle_B\bigr)
=(P_f|\psi\rangle_D)|0\rangle_C|\phi\rangle_B
\end{equation}
for arbitrary data states and arbitrary borrowed
ancilla states, including states entangled with
an unacted-upon external reference.
Let $\mathcal C$ be the terms of degree at
least four implemented by clean ladders,
let $t_3$ be the number of cubic terms,
and let $t_{4,B}$ be the number of quartics
implemented using a borrowed ancilla. Then
the exact gate counts of this construction are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:counts-pcc}
\begin{aligned}
N_{\rm CCZ}&=t_3+
\sum_{S\in\mathcal C}(2|S|-5)+4t_{4,B},\\
N_H&=4\sum_{S\in\mathcal C}(|S|-3)+4t_{4,B}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Its clean scratch requirement is
$\max_{S\in\mathcal C}(|S|-3)$, interpreted
as zero if $\mathcal C$ is empty.
At most one borrowed scratch qubit is required
for the sequential borrowed-quartic blocks.
The declared serial schedule has depth
$N_H+N_{\rm CCZ}$. These are construction
costs, not optimal gate-count or depth bounds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Canonical cancellation preserves the target
function because each Boolean monomial added
twice contributes zero modulo two. Every
cubic block contributes the required phase
without changing any bit. The clean-ladder
and borrowed-quartic identities proved
above each contribute their designated
monomial phase while restoring the
respective ancillary register \emph{exactly}.
Therefore the composition contributes
the XOR of all monomial phases, restores
each shared scratch register after every
block, and establishes \eqref{eq:pcccontract}
on a complete computational basis of
data and borrowed systems. Linearity proves
the assertion for all superpositions and
arbitrary external entanglement.
The compiler performs finitely many
operations per explicitly listed term,
so it terminates. Counting each block
and summing gives \eqref{eq:counts-pcc};
sequential reuse makes the clean capacity
a maximum rather than a sum.
\end{proof}

The compiler emits a machine-readable artifact
recording the source polynomial, the canonical
terms, a contiguous gate interval for each
block, its clean or borrowed ancilla role,
the circuit, exact gate counts and a digest.
The hash detects accidental changes but is
\emph{not} a mathematical proof and does not
authenticate a malicious compiler.

\begin{theorem}[Soundness of the restricted
independent certificate checker]\label{thm:checker}
Consider a certificate which is accepted by
the standalone checker under its declared
finite resource limits and exact
H--CCZ block grammar. Then the emitted
circuit implements precisely
\eqref{eq:pcccontract}, and the reported
gate counts and ancillary usage agree with
its gate list. The checker may decline
to certify otherwise valid H--CCZ circuits
outside its restricted grammar.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The checker does not import the compiler.
It parses each CCZ as the exact Boolean
phase $(-1)^{abc}$ and each contiguous
$H_t\mathrm{CCZ}_{abt}H_t$ as the exact
reversible assignment $t\mapsto t\oplus ab$.
These identities hold as quantum operators.
It maintains, over the Boolean ring
$\mathbb F_2[x_1,\ldots,x_N]/(x_i^2-x_i)$,
a polynomial for the value of each output
bit in terms of the original data and
borrowed input bits, along with an
accumulated phase polynomial.
Initial clean bits are represented by
zero, and borrowed bits by \emph{independent}
Boolean variables. XOR and Boolean
multiplication update these polynomials
exactly after each recognized gate.
Acceptance requires every block to restore
all wire polynomials, contribute exactly
its independently reconstructed target
monomial, use only its declared ancillary
wires, and match the separately counted
H and CCZ operations. The checker also
recomputes the source cancellation and
verifies that blocks partition the
entire circuit without gaps or trailing
gates. Thus the accepted circuit has
the desired action on every computational
basis vector satisfying the clean
precondition, with the entire borrowed
register unchanged. Operator linearity
gives \eqref{eq:pcccontract}.
Unrecognized Hadamard patterns are reported
as unsupported, not asserted incorrect.
\end{proof}

The implementation accepts explicit source
lists; therefore its running time and
output length are bounded in terms of the
\emph{input support size}, not necessarily
polynomial in $n$ for exponentially many
listed monomials. The current standalone
checker enforces a finite size cap and
does not certify arbitrary quantum
programs or general rewrite optimality.
The construction applies to ideal
H--CCZ gates, with no measurements or
device noise.

Proof-carrying quantum optimizers
and ancilla-safety verification have
substantial recent prior art.
VOQC is a formally verified quantum
circuit optimizer \cite{voqc};
AlchemQ studies per-result
equivalence certificates \cite{alchemq},
and Li and collaborators explicitly
study ancillary-state restoration
\cite{ancillasafe}. The present
scheme is narrower: its value is
an exactly checkable, small
phase-polynomial certificate format
with explicit clean/borrowed contracts,
not a claim of first discovery or
proof-assistant certification.


\section{Shared-prefix phase synthesis with temporary data borrowing}
\label{sec:shared-prefix}
For an explicitly given family of diagonal phase monomials
with a common prefix, scratch values need not be
recomputed for every term. Let
$p_1,\ldots,p_k$ be distinct data wires, $k\ge3$,
and let $T_1,\ldots,T_m$, $m\ge2$, be distinct
three-element subsets of the remaining data wires.
Their supports may overlap. Consider
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shared-target}
P_F|x\rangle=(-1)^{F(x)}|x\rangle,\qquad
F(x)=\bigoplus_{j=1}^{m}
\left(\prod_{\ell=1}^{k}x_{p_\ell}\right)
\left(\prod_{t\in T_j}x_t\right).
\end{equation}
The independent clean-ladder compiler
of Theorem~2, when applied separately to
each degree-$(k+3)$ monomial, requires
$k$ initialized scratch bits,
$m(2k+1)$ CCZ gates and $4km$ Hadamards.
The following improvement concerns
\emph{that specific earlier baseline};
stronger individual controlled-gate
decompositions exist \cite{khattar,lee}.

\begin{theorem}[Amortized shared-prefix construction]
\label{thm:shared-prefix}
The phase \eqref{eq:shared-target} can be realized
by an exact H--CCZ circuit using
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shared-counts}
N_{\mathrm{CCZ}}=2(k-1)+4m,\qquad
N_H=4(k-1)+4m,
\end{equation}
and $k-1$ clean scratch bits.
It allocates \emph{no} additional
unknown-state borrowed ancilla:
a temporarily borrowed wire is one
of the original prefix \emph{data} bits
and is restored exactly.
For every $k\ge3$, $m\ge2$, this
saves, relative to separate clean ladders,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shared-savings}
\Delta_{\mathrm{CCZ}}
=m(2k-3)-2(k-1)>0,\quad
\Delta_H=4(k-1)(m-1)>0,
\end{equation}
and one initialized and one total physical qubit.
The operator identity holds for arbitrary data
superpositions, including entanglement with
external systems.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compute the prefix conjunction
$A=\prod_{\ell=1}^{k}x_{p_\ell}$
into $k-1$ initially zero scratch bits
using the usual $k-1$ Toffoli ladder.
Keep its final scratch bit unchanged
while processing all suffixes.
For each $T_j=\{u,v,w\}$ apply the
compensated sequence
\[
\mathrm{CCX}_{A,u\to p_1},\quad
\mathrm{CCZ}_{p_1,v,w},\quad
\mathrm{CCX}_{A,u\to p_1},\quad
\mathrm{CCZ}_{p_1,v,w}.
\]
Here $A$ denotes the \emph{scratch wire}
holding the conjunction, not a second
use of the original data wire $p_1$.
The four active phase operands
$(A,u,v,w)$ are therefore distinct
from the borrowed target $p_1$.
By the full-operator borrowed-ancilla
identity, this block contributes
$(-1)^{Ax_ux_vx_w}$ and restores $p_1$
even when the prefix data and scratch
are already entangled. After all
$m$ blocks, reverse the conjunction
ladder to restore every clean scratch
bit. Their accumulated exponent
is exactly \eqref{eq:shared-target}.
There are $2(k-1)$ Toffolis outside
the borrowed-phase blocks and four
CCZ plus four Hadamard gates within
each such block, giving
\eqref{eq:shared-counts}.
Subtracting the independent-ladder
counts gives \eqref{eq:shared-savings};
their smallest positive CCZ saving is
two at $(k,m)=(3,2)$.
\end{proof}
For instance, when $k=4$ and $m=3$
the old compiler uses $27$ CCZ,
$48$ Hadamards and four clean scratch
bits. The construction above uses
$18$ CCZ, $24$ Hadamards and three
clean scratch bits, with no extra
borrowed physical qubit.

A separate proof-carrying implementation
of this restricted workload family
is contained in
\texttt{shared\_prefix\_compiler.py}.
Its independent checker,
\texttt{verify\_shared\_prefix.py},
replays the actual emitted gate list
using exact Boolean algebraic normal
forms with \emph{all original data bits
independent}, checks the phase polynomial,
returns every data and initialized
scratch expression to its prescribed
value, and recounts resources.
It imports no compiler routines.
A second exact-amplitude checker
verifies small instances directly.
The regression suite covers 262
certificate instances, 1408 full
basis-amplitude inputs and 14 altered
certificates rejected. A separate
symbolic stress case reaches 256
data wires. Finite checks support,
rather than replace, the general proof.

The construction is an amortized
improvement only relative to the
specified independent clean-ladder
baseline. Conditional-ancilla
methods already provide much smaller
clean-ancilla requirements for
individual multi-controlled gates
\cite{khattar,dutta,lee}. Sharing
compute--uncompute subexpressions
is also established optimization
practice, including the SPARE
compiler \cite{spare}. Neither historical
priority nor an optimum gate/resource
trade-off among all H--CCZ circuits
has been established here.

\section{Independent exact verification}
One standard-library checker simulates the actual gate matrices by maintaining signed integer amplitudes with a common factor $2^{-h/2}$. It checks the full clean-ancilla basis for n=3 through 12 (8,184 inputs), all 32 borrowed-ancilla basis inputs, 321 canonical-normal-form cases, H-count parity, and deliberate invalid rewrites. A separately implemented checker uses the exact 2-by-2 Toffoli conjugation identity and Boolean XOR operations rather than simulating Hadamards; it checks n=3 through 14 (32,760 inputs), the borrowed identity and Boolean normal forms. Finite verification reinforces rather than replaces the general inductive proofs.

The proof-carrying suite independently certifies
112 family and randomized source instances,
compares 1,712 actual exact-amplitude basis
executions with the expected phase, and
rejects 12 deliberately altered artifacts,
including mutations whose digests were
recomputed. The certificate checker is
separate from the compiler and does not
use the compiler's expected gate templates.
These are ideal-gate and precisely scoped:
a general-purpose quantum compiler,
formal Lean checker, gate-optimality
theorem, and hardware-cost model have
not been constructed. Generalized Toffoli synthesis and clean/dirty ancilla techniques have established priority \cite{barenco,khattar}; no unprecedented quantum algorithm is claimed.
