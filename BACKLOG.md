## Selection rule

Prefer well-posed theorems whose boundary is sharp and whose calculation can be independently repeated. Publish negative results if they conclusively rule out a meaningful, clearly defined mechanism. Do not relabel an unresolved grand challenge as solved merely because a restricted lemma is proved.
